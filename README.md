# 🎓 AI Campus Chaos

### An AI-assisted interactive game about surviving MBA life.

AI Campus Chaos is a fun, browser-based decision game where everyday MBA situations become a series of difficult — and sometimes ridiculous — choices.

Every decision affects four aspects of your campus life:

📚 Grades · 😴 Sleep · 🤝 Network · 🧠 Sanity

Your goal?

Survive the semester without completely losing it.

## 💡 The Idea

The prompt was simple:

> Use an AI tool to create something fun, unique and interesting.

Instead of creating a conventional AI-generated image, poem or song, I wanted to build something interactive.

The concept combines:

- 🤖 AI-assisted ideation
- 💻 JavaScript
- 🎮 Game mechanics
- 🎨 UI/UX
- 📖 Storytelling

The result is a small decision engine that turns familiar MBA experiences into a playable simulation.

---

## 🕹️ How It Works

The player faces 5 campus situations.

### 1. ⏰ The 8 AM Class

You slept for four hours.

Do you:

- Attend class?
- Sleep?
- Sit in the back?

---

### 2. 📊 The Case Competition

A case competition suddenly appears with a deadline tonight.

Do you:

- Take the lead?
- Delegate smartly?
- Pretend you never saw it?

---

### 3. 🔋 3% Battery

Your phone is dying and your charger is far away.

Do you:

- Run for the charger?
- Ask a friend?
- Accept your fate?

---

### 4. 🤝 Placement Talk

Everyone is networking.

You have exactly one sentence prepared.

Do you:

- Introduce yourself?
- Ask a thoughtful question?
- Leave early?

---

### 5. 🎤 Final Presentation

The projector stops working.

47 people are looking at you.

Do you:

- Debug it live?
- Use the backup PDF?
- Blame technology?

---

## 📈 Player Stats

| Attribute | Represents |
|---|---|
| 📚 Grades | Academic performance |
| 😴 Sleep | Physical recovery |
| 🤝 Network | Networking & relationships |
| 🧠 Sanity | Mental well-being |
